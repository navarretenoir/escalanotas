Made for calculating school grades in Chile. Builds the 1.0–7.0 scale for any passing threshold and maximum score, tells which grade a score gets and how many points a grade needs, and computes a whole class with average, median, pass rate and distribution.

Exports to Excel, Word, CSV, image and PDF. Works offline, with light (grid notebook) and dark (chalkboard) themes. No ads, no trackers. Android 4.0+. (Interface in Spanish.)

Developed by Javiera Navarrete.
